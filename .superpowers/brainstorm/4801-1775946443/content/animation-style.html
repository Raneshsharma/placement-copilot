Animation style: how much motion?

These represent a spectrum — from subtle polish to ambient energy

A

Subtle & Purposeful

Scroll-triggered fades (opacity 0→1, 300ms ease). Nothing bounces or loops. Motion serves comprehension, not decoration. Clean, professional, fast.

  • • Elements fade in as you scroll
  • • Hover states shift opacity/underline
  • • No ambient motion or particle effects
B

Layered & Ambient

Staggered reveals (elements cascade in sequence), floating decorative elements, subtle parallax depth. Motion creates energy and draws the eye forward.

  • • Staggered card entrance animations
  • • Floating decorative shapes (subtle, 1-2 per section)
  • • Parallax layers on hero
  • • Scroll-triggered counter animations (metrics count up)
C

Dynamic & Cinematic

Heavy motion: looping ambient animations, particle effects, morphing shapes, spring physics, screen-filling transitions. The page feels alive. High impact, higher risk of distraction.

  • • Particle background (like current page)
  • • Spring animations with overshoot
  • • Morphing icons and shapes
  • • Section transitions that slide/fold
  • • Micro-interactions on every button

My recommendation: Layered & Ambient — it matches the "polished movement without distraction" brief while giving the page energy and depth. Subtle is clean but can feel static; Dynamic risks overwhelming the message.
