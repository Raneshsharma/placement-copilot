Feature storytelling: what's the narrative arc?

Pick the structure that best fits your audience's mindset

A

Job seeker journey

Sections follow the search arc: confused → building → optimizing → getting hired. Mirror the mental state of your visitor at each stage.

Structure:
"You feel lost" → "Build with AI" → "Optimize for ATS" → "Land interviews" → "Track progress"
B

Problem → Solution

Lead with pain: ATS filters your resume out. Recruiters spend 6 seconds. Wrong keywords. Then show how each feature solves it.

Structure:
Pain point framing → Feature cards that directly address each pain → Outcome (your profile wins)
C

Capability showcase

Grid of 8-10 features with icons, each with a one-line value prop. Let visitors self-select what matters to them.

Structure:
Clean grid: AI Builder, ATS Scanner, Role Matcher, Skill Gap, Interview Prep, Tracker, LinkedIn Import, Consulting
D

Interactive workflow

Single interactive path — user clicks through a mini walkthrough of what building their profile looks like. Show, don't list.

Structure:
Interactive tab/stepper: "Upload or import" → "AI builds your draft" → "Tailor for a role" → "Get ATS score" → CTA

My recommendation: Job seeker journey — it tells a story the visitor is already living, making every section feel relevant rather than a feature pitch.
